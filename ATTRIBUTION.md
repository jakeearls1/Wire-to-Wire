# Data attribution

Wire to Wire's planned historical-statistics layer uses the SABR Lahman Baseball Database.

- Database: Lahman Baseball Database
- Creator: Sean Lahman
- Maintainer/publisher: Society for American Baseball Research (SABR)
- Coverage targeted by this project: 1871–2025 release
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0)
- Official source: https://sabr.org/lahman-database/

Team/player names and historical facts are used for identification in the prototype. MLB/team logos and player photography are intentionally not bundled.
